Privacy policy

rclone backup is used only by its owner. It requests the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, which only allows access to files this app itself creates.

It uploads the owner's own encrypted backup files to the owner's own Google Drive. It does not read other Drive files, and does not collect, sell or share any personal data. No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive.

The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account settings (Security, Third-party access).
